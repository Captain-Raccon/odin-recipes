Creamy Chicken

Description

This creamy chicken enchilada casserole comes together with just five ingredients and a genius shortcut. Rotisserie chicken, salsa verde, and jarred Alfredo sauce make it rich, flavorful, and perfect for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 ounce) jar Alfredo sauce
  • 1 (16 ounce) jar salsa verde
  • 12 (6 inch) flour or corn tortillas, cut in half
  • 5 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 8 ounces Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
  • chopped fresh cilantro and chopped tomatoes for garnish, optional

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Combine Alfredo sauce and salsa verde in a bowl.
  3. Arrange eight tortilla halves in the prepared baking dish, to mostly cover the bottom of the dish.
  4. Spread 1/3 of cooked chicken evenly over the tortillas. Pour 1/3 of Alfredo mixture on top of cooked chicken, then sprinkle with 2/3 cup of Monterey Jack cheese. Repeat the layers 2 more times, ending with the remaining cheese on top.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until heated through and the cheese is melted, about 25 minutes.
  6. Let stand 5 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh cilantro and tomatoes, if desired.
